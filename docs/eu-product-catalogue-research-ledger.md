# EU Aesthetic Product Catalogue — Research Ledger

**Research status:** Discovery and evidence collection. This ledger separates market visibility from patient-ready or distribution-ready status. A product is not permitted into consent assembly solely because it is sold online or mentioned in a clinic's catalogue.

## Evidence hierarchy

| Tier | Evidence type | Permitted use in Aegis Consent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official regulator or public registry record, including marketing-authorisation or medical-device evidence | Supports classification and jurisdiction decision-making; does not itself create consent content. |
| 2 | Original manufacturer ChPL/SPC, IFU/DFU, prescribing information, or controlled product documentation | May support exact, language-matched disclosure excerpts after administrator verification. |
| 3 | Authorised distributor documentation with traceable manufacturer and identifier evidence | Supply-chain due diligence only until corroborated with tier 1 or tier 2 evidence. |
| Excluded | Marketplace listings, social media posts, reseller marketing, price pages, and training content | Discovery leads only; never a source for clinical disclosure or regulatory status. |
